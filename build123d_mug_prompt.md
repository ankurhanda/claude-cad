# Build123d CAD Prompt — Speckled Ceramic Mug

Use this as a **text-to-CAD prompt** for Build123d. The goal is to recreate a **minimal ceramic mug** with a softly rounded base, nearly cylindrical upper body, thick lip, and a smooth loop handle. Model the geometry first; treat the surface speckling as a visual/material note rather than embossed geometry unless explicitly requested.

## Overall object description

Create a **stoneware-style coffee mug** with these characteristics:

- Overall form is a **tall rounded mug** with a vertical body that is almost cylindrical through the upper 70–75% of its height.
- The **bottom transitions into a smooth hemispherical / bullet-like rounding**, rather than ending in a flat sharp-edged cylinder.
- The mug has a **slightly thickened rim** with a soft outer roundover and a matching inner roundover.
- Wall thickness is fairly uniform and substantial, typical of ceramic mugs.
- The handle is a **large vertical loop** attached near the upper sidewall and lower sidewall, with a generous finger clearance and an oval-to-round cross-section.
- Exterior appearance is **matte gray ceramic with irregular darker speckles**, but this should be represented as appearance only, not physical bumps.

## Recommended real-world dimensions

Use dimensions in **millimeters**. Target a realistic medium mug size:

- Overall mug height: **102 mm**
- Outer diameter at upper body: **82 mm**
- Inner diameter at opening: **72 mm**
- Wall thickness near straight sidewall: **5 mm**
- Base outer width at lowest rounded region: **50–55 mm visual contact region**, but body should smoothly blend from full diameter into rounded bottom
- Rim thickness at lip: **5–6 mm**
- Internal depth from rim to lowest interior point: **92 mm**

Handle:

- Overall handle outer height: **68 mm**
- Overall handle outer width from mug wall to farthest outer edge: **36 mm**
- Clear inner opening height: **48 mm**
- Clear inner opening width: **22 mm**
- Handle cross-section: **elliptical**, about **11 mm wide × 14 mm deep**
- Upper handle attachment center located about **16 mm below top rim**
- Lower handle attachment center located about **24 mm above the lowest exterior point**

These values can vary by ±2–4 mm as long as proportions remain the same.

## Geometric intent

### 1. Body silhouette

The mug body should read as:

- **Top section:** straight, almost vertical cylindrical wall
- **Lower section:** gently tapering inward through curvature only, not a linear draft
- **Bottom:** fully rounded exterior, like the lower half of a capsule blended into the body

Important:
- Do **not** make the mug a simple cylinder with a flat base.
- Do **not** add feet, rings, or a pedestal.
- The mug should look stable, but the visible form should end in a **continuous rounded underside**.
- The profile should feel soft and hand-formed, but still clean and symmetric.

A good side profile can be generated by revolving a 2D sketch with:
- a vertical wall for most of the height,
- then a large tangent arc inward,
- then a smaller closing arc to the lowest point.

### 2. Interior cavity

The interior should:

- Follow the outer shape, but with a **flatter and less bulbous bottom transition** than the exterior.
- Maintain approximately **5 mm wall thickness** on the sides.
- Maintain approximately **6–8 mm material thickness** under the lowest interior point.
- Have a soft inner fillet where the sidewall transitions into the bottom.
- Be open at the top with a gently rounded lip.

### 3. Rim

The rim should be:

- Circular and level
- Thick enough to look ceramic and sturdy
- Softly rounded on both inside and outside edges
- Not flared outward significantly; keep the mug mouth almost straight-walled

Suggested edge treatment:
- outer top edge fillet: **1.8–2.2 mm**
- inner top edge fillet: **1.2–1.8 mm**

### 4. Handle

The handle should be a smooth vertical loop with these properties:

- Starts near the upper sidewall just below the rim
- Ends around the lower-middle to lower sidewall
- Has a **C-shaped side view**
- Outer contour is smooth and continuous
- Inner opening is vertically elongated
- Cross-section should be an ellipse or rounded rectangle, thicker in the depth direction for comfort
- Attachments should blend gently into the mug body with generous fillets

Handle orientation/details:
- Handle lies in a vertical plane tangent to the mug side
- Maximum handle offset from the mug wall: about **30–36 mm**
- The top attachment should be shorter and more horizontal before curving outward
- The lower attachment should sweep in smoothly toward the body
- The lowest point of the handle sits slightly above the mug’s lowest point

Suggested handle modeling method:
- Sketch a side spline or tangent-arc path for the loop centerline
- Sweep an elliptical profile along that path
- Boolean union with body
- Add blend fillets at both attachments in the **5–8 mm** range

## Construction strategy for Build123d

Model in this order:

1. Create the **outer revolved body** from a side profile sketch.
2. Create the **inner cavity** by revolving a second profile and subtracting it.
3. Add top-edge fillets for the rim.
4. Build the handle as a **swept solid** from a 2D side path.
5. Union the handle to the mug.
6. Apply large blend fillets where handle meets body.
7. Assign ceramic material / matte gray appearance.
8. Optionally add speckled texture in rendering only.

## Side profile guidance for revolved outer body

Construct a right-half profile around the central vertical axis:

- Start at the top outer rim radius: **41 mm**
- Go straight downward for about **68–72 mm**
- Begin an inward convex curve
- Blend into a rounded bottom apex centered on axis
- Total height should end at **102 mm**

A useful profile concept:

- Segment A: vertical line from `(41, 102)` to `(41, 34)`
- Segment B: tangent arc inward to around `(31, 10)`
- Segment C: closing tangent arc to bottom center `(0, 0)`

Then revolve 360°.

## Side profile guidance for inner cavity

Inner profile:

- Start at top inner radius: **36 mm**
- Descend mostly vertically
- Curve inward more gently than exterior
- End above the outer bottom to preserve ceramic thickness

Example concept:

- Segment A: vertical line from `(36, 97)` to `(36, 22)`
- Segment B: inward tangent arc to around `(24, 8)`
- Segment C: closing arc to `(0, 8)`

Revolve and subtract.

## Build123d-oriented modeling instructions

Ask the CAD generator to use these operations explicitly:

- `Sketch` and side-profile construction on an XZ or YZ plane
- `make_face()` or equivalent closed profile generation
- `revolve(axis=Axis.Z)` or equivalent full revolution
- separate inner and outer revolved solids
- boolean subtraction for hollowing
- `fillet()` on lip edges
- `sweep()` for the handle from a planar path
- boolean union for handle attachment
- blend / fillet on handle-body intersections

## Surface finish and appearance notes

- Material appearance: **matte light-to-medium cool gray ceramic**
- Add irregular **desaturated darker gray / taupe speckles** randomly distributed over the exterior
- Speckles should vary from **1 mm to 6 mm** visually
- Speckle density should be moderate and uneven
- Finish should feel like handcrafted stoneware, but geometry should remain rotationally clean and symmetric

Do not model:
- logos
- embossed text
- grooves
- flat base ring
- sharp chamfers
- angular handle

## Negative constraints

Avoid these common mistakes:

- making the mug a straight cylinder with a flat bottom
- making the base too spherical, like a ball
- making the rim thin like glass
- making the handle too small or too circular
- making the handle attach at the extreme top and bottom
- using a sharp-edged torus-like handle
- tapering the body linearly from top to bottom
- adding a foot ring unless explicitly required

## Concise one-block prompt

Create a ceramic coffee mug in Build123d with a rotationally symmetric body approximately 102 mm tall and 82 mm outer diameter at the top. The upper body is nearly cylindrical with straight vertical sides, while the lower third transitions smoothly inward into a softly rounded bullet-shaped bottom rather than a flat base. Hollow the mug with roughly 5 mm wall thickness and a gently rounded interior bottom. Add a thick rounded rim with soft inner and outer fillets. Attach a large comfortable vertical loop handle on one side using a swept elliptical profile; the handle should have about 68 mm outer height, about 36 mm projection from the mug wall, and a vertically elongated inner opening. Blend the handle smoothly into the mug body with generous fillets. Finish as matte gray stoneware with irregular darker speckles as render texture only, not embossed geometry.
