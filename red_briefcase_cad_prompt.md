# CAD Description: Red Parts Briefcase / Organizer

Accurate description of the **as-built CAD model** of a red hard-plastic portable organizer briefcase with a black internal bin tray and open lid.

---

## 1) Overall object identity and design intent

A **hard-plastic portable organizer / briefcase-style parts case** consisting of:

- a **rectangular lower tub** in bright red plastic,
- a **matching red hinged lid** opened to approximately 100 degrees,
- a **grey front carry-handle / latch assembly** centered on the front wall,
- a **removable black internal tray insert** subdivided into 8 open-top rectangular bins,
- molded geometric details typical of injection-molded storage organizers (fillets, chamfers, draft, ribs, pockets).

The case is wider than it is deep, in a landscape orientation like a shallow tool briefcase.

---

## 2) Master dimensions

### Outer closed-case envelope

- **Overall width (X):** 500 mm
- **Overall depth (Y):** 340 mm
- **Overall closed height (Z):** 145 mm (base 95 mm + lid 50 mm)

### Lower half (base tub)

- **Base outer height:** 95 mm
- **Wall thickness:** 3.5 mm
- **Bottom thickness:** 2.0 mm (thin floor; tray has its own floor plate)
- **Outer corner radius:** 12.0 mm (vertical edges)
- **Inner corner radius:** 7.0 mm (vertical edges)
- **Top edge fillet:** 3.0 mm
- **Interior dimensions:** 493 mm wide x 333 mm deep

### Lid

- **Lid outer dimensions:** 498 mm wide x 338 mm deep x 50 mm tall
- **Lid wall thickness:** 3.5 mm
- **Inner cavity depth:** 42.5 mm
- **Lid corner radius:** 11.0 mm (vertical edges)
- **Top edge fillet:** 3.0 mm

- **Tray width:** 493 mm (fills base interior exactly)
- **Tray depth:** 333 mm (fills base interior exactly)
- **Tray height:** 60 mm
- **Tray sits on base floor** at Z = 2.0 mm (base bottom thickness)

## 3) Base shell geometry

### 3.1 Construction

The base is a rectangular tub created from a rounded-rectangle planform extruded to 95 mm, then hollowed by subtracting an inner cavity starting at Z = 2 mm (floor thickness). The outer vertical edges are filleted at 12 mm radius, inner at 7 mm, and the top rim edges at 3 mm.

### 3.2 Front wall details

- **Angular recessed pockets** flanking the handle zone: two 65 mm wide x 22 mm tall cutouts at X = +/-129.5 mm, near the top of the front wall. Edges are chamfered at 6 mm.
- **Horizontal step line** at 35% of base height, spanning nearly the full width (470 mm), recessed 1.5 mm deep x 2 mm tall.

### 3.3 Side walls

Each side wall has a **slot-like molded detail**: 80 mm wide x 4 mm tall rectangular recess at 55% of base height, cut 3 mm deep into the wall.

### 3.4 Lid seating rim

Four thin red border strips (4 mm wide, 5 mm tall) run around the top perimeter of the base at Z = 90 mm. The rim footprint is inset 4 mm from the case outer edge (492 mm x 332 mm). Corner strips do not overlap.

### 3.5 Bottom feet

Four molded feet (22 x 14 x 2.5 mm) at the bottom corners, positioned 25 mm inward from the width edges and 22 mm inward from the depth edges.

### 3.6 Corner bosses

Two small cylindrical bosses (radius 4 mm, length 2 mm) on the front face near the left and right corners, at 50% height. Oriented perpendicular to the front wall.

---

## 4) Lid geometry

### 4.1 Overall shape

The lid is a shallow rectangular shell (498 x 338 x 50 mm) with filleted vertical edges (11 mm radius) and top-edge fillets (3 mm). The interior is hollowed to a 42.5 mm cavity (482 x 322 mm inner footprint, 8 mm frame all around).

### 4.2 Right end bevel

An angled cut at 25 degrees removes material from the right end of the lid, giving it a distinctive tapered silhouette.

### 4.3 Grip bosses

Two raised rectangular islands on the lid's outer surface at X = -60 mm and X = +60 mm, Y = +30 mm:
- Each boss: 70 x 22 x 10 mm with filleted vertical edges (4 mm) and filleted top edges (3 mm).
- Each has a **recessed center**: 58 x 16 x 4 mm pocket cut into the top.

### 4.4 Inner stiffening ribs

Two vertical ribs (2.5 mm thick x 310 mm long x 21.25 mm tall) run along the inside of the lid at X = -225 mm and X = +225 mm.

### 4.5 Open position

The lid is rotated 100 degrees open around the hinge axis at the rear of the case. In the open position, the lid leans back from the base with its inner face visible.

---

## 5) Hinge system

### 5.1 Placement

The hinge axis runs parallel to the width (X-axis) at Y = 168 mm (near rear edge), Z = 92 mm (near top of base).

### 5.2 Construction

Three hinge barrel groups located at X = -210, 0, +210 mm. Each group consists of:
- **Barrel:** cylinder, radius 5 mm, length 30 mm, colored red.
- **Pin:** cylinder, radius 2 mm, length 34 mm, colored silver.

Both barrel and pin are oriented along the X-axis.

---

## 6) Front handle / latch assembly (grey)

### 6.1 Placement

Centered on the front face (Y = -170 mm), spanning 170 mm in width.

### 6.2 Components

1. **Main housing plate:** 170 x 14 x 72 mm rectangular block, centered vertically on the front wall, slightly proud of the surface. Vertical edges filleted at 3.5 mm.

2. **Drawer-pull lower section:** 160 x 18 x 28 mm block below the main housing, with 3 mm filleted vertical edges.

3. **Horizontal ribs:** Four 150 x 2 x 2.5 mm ribs spaced 7 mm apart starting at Z = 22 mm, mounted on the face of the housing.

4. **Support pillars (left and right):** 24 x 16 x 34 mm blocks at X = +/-67 mm, rising from the top of the base. Top edges filleted at 6 mm.

5. **Carry bar:** 156 x 16 x 14 mm horizontal bar bridging the two support pillars, 20 mm above the base top. Horizontal and vertical edges filleted (7 mm and 4 mm respectively).

6. **Latch clasps:** Two 12 x 6 x 10 mm blocks at X = +/-77 mm near the base bottom (Z = 5 mm).

## 10) Construction sequence (as built)

1. Base outer rounded box (500 x 340 x 95 mm, corner radius 12 mm).
2. Hollow the base by subtracting inner cavity (2 mm floor, 3.5 mm walls).
3. Cut front angular pockets and horizontal step line.
4. Cut side wall slot details.
5. Add lid seating rim strips, bottom feet, and corner bosses.
6. Build hinge barrels and pins at rear edge.
7. Build lid shell (498 x 338 x 50 mm), hollow interior, bevel right end.
8. Add lid grip bosses with recessed centers, inner stiffening ribs.
9. Position lid at base top, rotate 100 degrees open around hinge axis.
10. Build grey handle housing, drawer pull, ribs, support pillars, carry bar, latch clasps.
11. Build black tray: floor plate, outer rim shell.
12. Add 2 full-depth vertical dividers (column separators).
13. Add 2 horizontal dividers in left column, 1 in middle column, 2 in right column.
14. Assign colors and assemble all 42 parts into final compound.
